# Executive Summary

## Project

Sales Performance BI Dashboard

## Business Context

This project analyzes sample sales activity across regions, product categories, customer segments, and sales channels. The goal is to demonstrate how business intelligence can turn raw sales records into executive-level insights.

## Key Findings

1. **Enterprise customers generate the highest-value orders.**
   The sample data shows stronger revenue concentration in enterprise and mid-market segments compared with smaller accounts.

2. **Software and services drive the strongest revenue potential.**
   Analytics products and implementation packages create larger individual order values than lower-ticket items.

3. **Regional performance should be reviewed monthly.**
   Revenue varies by region, so leadership should monitor whether regional growth is consistent or dependent on only a few large orders.

4. **Direct sales appears strategically important.**
   Direct channel orders include several high-value deals, making it a priority channel for deeper analysis.

## Recommended Actions

- Prioritize enterprise and mid-market sales motions.
- Track monthly revenue by region and category.
- Monitor average order value by customer segment.
- Compare direct, partner, and online sales channels.
- Expand dashboard filters for region, category, and channel.
